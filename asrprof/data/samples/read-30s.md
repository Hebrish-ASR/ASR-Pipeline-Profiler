Every weekday the first train leaves the station just before six. The platform is quiet at that hour, and most of the people waiting already know each other by sight, if not by name.

By the third stop the carriage is full. Somebody opens a newspaper, somebody else starts a phone call, and the quiet is gone for the rest of the journey.
