The kitchen tap had been dripping for about two weeks before I finally decided to deal with it. At first it was only a drop every few seconds, but by the end it was keeping me awake at night, and the water bill was starting to show it.

I watched a couple of short videos, turned off the water under the sink, and took the handle apart. The problem turned out to be a small rubber washer that had cracked down the middle. It cost less than a cup of coffee to replace.

Putting everything back together took longer than taking it apart, mostly because I had not paid attention to the order of the pieces. Next time I will take a photo before I start. The tap has been silent ever since.
